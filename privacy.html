Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

This policy explains what information the Footvolly game ("the game", "we") uses, why, and the choices you have. Contact: levieyal1221@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Game account identifiers. When you play, the game signs you in to Unity Gaming Services, either with your Apple Game Center account or anonymously. This creates a player ID. If you use Game Center, Apple shares your Game Center player ID and nickname with the game.
  • Game progress. Coins, trophies, leagues, owned items, World Tour results, Tour Pass progress, your team name, player names and looks are saved on your device and on your account (Unity Cloud Save) so they can be restored on a new device.
  • Online play data. During online matches the game exchanges gameplay data (movements, ball, score) with other players through Unity Relay servers. Like any internet connection this involves your IP address. Other players see your team name, player name or Game Center nickname, looks and trophies.
  • Leaderboards. Your trophies are submitted to the Game Center leaderboard, where other players can see your nickname and score.
  • Purchases. In-app purchases are processed by Apple. We never see your payment details; we only receive confirmation of what was bought.

What we do not do

We do not sell your data, we do not show third-party advertising and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites. We do not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts or photos.

Service providers

Unity Technologies (authentication, cloud save, online multiplayer and in-app purchase handling) and Apple (Game Center, App Store purchases) process data on our behalf, under their own privacy policies: Unity, Apple.

Computer-controlled opponents

When no other player is available, online search may match you with a computer-controlled team.

Keeping and deleting data

Your progress is kept while you have an account. You can delete it at any time in the game (Settings › DELETE MY DATA), which removes your cloud save and online account, or by emailing us. Game Center data is managed by Apple in your iPhone settings.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from children beyond the game identifiers described above.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
