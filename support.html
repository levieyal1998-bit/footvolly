Common questions

My progress is gone after reinstalling. Sign in to Game Center on your iPhone (Settings › Game Center) with the same Apple ID and open the game again: your progress comes back from your account.

A purchase did not arrive. Open SHOP › RESTORE PURCHASES. If it still does not appear, email us with the date of the purchase. Payments are handled by Apple; refunds are requested at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Online matches feel slow. The connection pill under the score shows your ping. Wi-Fi or a stronger mobile signal helps. If nobody is available, you may be matched with a computer-controlled opponent.

Delete my data. Settings › DELETE MY DATA erases your progress and online account. You can also email us to ask for deletion.
